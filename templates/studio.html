ClassMitra Studio

Type your lesson or upload a file. You get slides with images (and a PowerPoint file), a spoken explanation, and a teacher who explains it.

Allowed files: PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), PDF (.pdf), images (.png .jpg .jpeg .webp) and text (.txt .md). Up to 15 MB. Old .ppt and .doc files are not supported: save them as .pptx or .docx first.

1. Slides

Click to select · drag to move · drag the blue corner to resize · double-click text to edit · Delete key removes

2. Spoken explanation

3. Teacher teaches your slides

The teacher explains each slide on the screen, pointing at the text with a laser pointer. After every slide he asks one question about it. The next slide starts only after you answer correctly.
